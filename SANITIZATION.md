# Sanitization policy

This repository is public. The following rules apply to everything committed here.

## Allowed

- Public program names, handles, public HackerOne URLs, program type, and the
  registrable domains that appear in the program's published scope.
- Generic methodology and defensive lessons with no target-specific detail.
- Finding summaries **only after** the program discloses the report, publishes an
  advisory, or gives written permission to publish.
- Public references such as a CVE, GHSA, NVD entry, or a disclosed HackerOne report.

## Not allowed

- Report bodies, proof-of-concept steps, payloads, raw requests or responses.
- Screenshots, videos, HAR files, logs, dumps, archives, or any other evidence.
- Cookies, tokens, API keys, credentials, session identifiers, or hashes.
- Customer data, personal data, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, or account
  identifiers.
- Internal hostnames, private endpoints, internal IP addresses, or network topology.
- Unpublished exploitation details, chained attack recipes, or target-specific paths.
- Private, invite-only, or unverifiable programs — not even their names.

## Disclosure rule

A finding stays private until the program discloses it or grants permission. A report
being submitted, triaged, resolved, or paid does **not** by itself grant permission to
publish it. When in doubt, ask the program first.
